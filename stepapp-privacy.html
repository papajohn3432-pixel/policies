StepApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

StepApp ("the app") is published by Babar Imran ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: Papajohn3432@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your goals, workouts, walks, weight entries, settings and walk routes are stored on your phone. Walk routes (GPS tracks) are never uploaded.

Information we collect

  • Account details (only if you sign in): your email address and display name, handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). You can also use the app as a guest without an account.
  • Fitness data: daily steps, distance, calories, active minutes, workouts and streaks. With a signed-in account this summary data is backed up to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore so it follows you to a new phone.
  • Step data from your phone: read from your phone's step sensor and, if you allow it, from Health Connect (steps only). We never write to Health Connect.
  • Location: used only while you are recording a walk, to measure distance and draw your route on your device. There is no background location collection.
  • Groups and challenges: if you create or join a group, your display name, step counts, streak and chat messages are visible to the other members of that group.
  • Usage and crash data: anonymous app-usage events and crash reports through Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics, used to fix bugs and improve the app.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive only whether a subscription is active, never your payment details.

How we use it

To provide the features you use (step counting, walks, plans, sync, groups and challenges), keep your data backed up, understand app problems, and provide subscriptions. We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.

Sharing

Data is processed by Google (Firebase, Google Play) as our service provider. Group data is shared only with members of your group. We do not share your data with anyone else except where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account to remove your account and your cloud copy of your data. You can also email us to request deletion. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

StepApp is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Health disclaimer

StepApp provides general fitness information and is not a medical service. Talk to a professional before starting a new exercise programme.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted, and cloud access is restricted so each user can read and write only their own data (and their own groups' shared data).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
